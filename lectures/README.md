# How lectures work

Each course has scheduled lecture slots on the semester timetable in [semesters/timetables/](../semesters/timetables/). They work like university classes, except that you can attend at the scheduled time or later.

## Attending a lecture

1. When the slot comes (or whenever you're ready that day), open the course thread and write the course and lecture number, for example **"PHY 101 L5"**.
2. I teach the lecture live. It is an interactive set of lecture notes, built from the sources in the syllabus and designed to keep your cognitive load in the productive zone (see the [Teaching Method](../docs/teaching-method.md)). It runs in this order:
   - **Prerequisite warm-up** (5 min): two or three quick retrieval questions on the pieces today's topic depends on.
   - **Learning objectives**: what you should be able to do afterwards.
   - **The lecture itself, in chunks of one new idea each**: the idea and its derivation in small steps, then **a fully worked example straight away**, then a **concept check** to answer before reading on. The lecture adapts to your answers. Figures carry their own labels, and equations are restated where they are used, so you never have to flip back.
   - **A paired problem** that mirrors the last worked example.
   - **Summary**, plus the **matching sources**: the MIT OCW video lecture and textbook pages covering the same material, used as a second explanation.
   - **Load check**: rate the lecture's mental effort from 1 to 9 and say where you got lost, if anywhere. Your answer sets the pace of the next lecture.
3. Interrupt at any time with questions, as you would in a small class.
4. After the lecture, the notes are saved here as `lectures/<semester>/<course>/L<nn>.md`. That way you build your own set of course notes for revision.

## Recitations, labs and tutorials

These work the same way. Write **"PHY 101 Recitation W3"**, **"CMP 101 Lab W3"** or **"Tutorial PHY 101 PS2"** in the thread.

## If you miss a slot

That's fine. Attend the class later the same week. What you must not do is fall more than a week behind, because problem sets are due every Sunday. The Monday weekly release will remind you which classes are scheduled that week.
