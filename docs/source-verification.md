# Source Verification

This document records what each of the five uploaded syllabus PDFs is, whether it is official and current, and which better official sources were checked (October 2026).

## 1. The five uploaded PDFs

| File | What it actually is | Official? | Current? | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `mit_phy_syllabus.pdf` (28 pp.) | The **Department of Physics** section of the **MIT Course Catalog** (catalog.mit.edu): programme description, Focused and Flexible options, minors, faculty list, and every undergraduate and graduate subject description. PDF metadata shows it was generated by CourseLeaf, MIT's catalog system, on 16 Oct 2026 | **Yes** | **Yes** (2026–27 catalog) | Authoritative. Use it |
| `mit_phy_catalog.pdf` (21 pp.) | The same MIT catalog section, subjects only, generated the same day | **Yes** | **Yes** | A duplicate subset of the file above |
| `harvardspringphy.pdf` (8 pp.) | Harvard course listings for **Spring 2024 only** (Physical Sciences 3, 12A, 70; Physics 15A–C, 90R, 91R, 95, 113, 129, 143A/B, 153, 175, 181 …), copied from Harvard's course search into Word | Official course text | **No.** Two years old and covers one term only | Useful for course descriptions. Not a curriculum |
| `harvard_phy_course.pdf` (58 pp.) | ***Physics Course Guide*, 2025–2026 edition**, written by the **Harvard-Radcliffe Society of Physics Students** (SPS) | **No.** Student-written. The guide itself says to check my.harvard and the Student Handbook before deciding anything | **Yes** | Very valuable for sequencing advice and candid course opinions. Not authoritative |
| `UniversityofDhakaPHYcourses.pdf` (35 pp.) | University of Dhaka, Department of Physics, **Four-Year B.S. (Honours) syllabus, effective from session 2015–16** | **Yes.** It is byte-for-byte identical (same MD5) to the PDF linked from [du.ac.bd/undergrad/PHY](https://www.du.ac.bd/undergrad/PHY) | It is the latest version DU publishes, but it dates from 2015 | Authoritative for DU. There is one inconsistency, noted below |

**DU note.** The syllabus states that **136 credits** are required, but the year-wise course lists on the DU page add up to about **126**. Year 1 is listed as 34 credits but its courses total 28, and Year 2 is listed as 35 but totals 33. The gap is probably non-major courses that are counted differently. This doesn't affect our design, but it is worth knowing if you ever compare transcripts with DU.

## 3. Documents worth downloading yourself

Some sites block automated access, so these are worth opening in your own browser and saving to `docs/reference/`:

1. **Oxford Physics Undergraduate Course Handbook** (current year), from the Oxford Physics Department's current-students pages. It has the full Part A/B/C syllabuses and the problem-sheet culture.
2. **Princeton Undergraduate Announcement, Physics** (link above). It has the binding departmental requirements.
3. **Harvard Student Handbook, Fields of Concentration: Physics**. It has the binding concentration rules.
4. **Caltech Catalog PDF, Section Four (Courses)**. It has full Ph course descriptions.

None of these changes the curriculum's structure. They only add detail to individual syllabi.

## 4. How the sources were weighted

- **Sequence and prerequisites:** MIT, the most explicit and best-documented.
- **Depth and breadth of the core:** Oxford Parts A and B, which are the broadest compulsory core.
- **Independent work:** Princeton (Junior Papers, thesis) and MIT (thesis, Junior Lab).
- **Computing and labs:** Caltech, Harvard and MIT 8.13/8.16.
- **Regional topics and annual viva:** Dhaka.
- **Free materials:** MIT OCW, Feynman Lectures (Caltech), David Tong (Cambridge), Binney (Oxford), Georgi and Morin (Harvard). Details are in the [Library](library.md).
