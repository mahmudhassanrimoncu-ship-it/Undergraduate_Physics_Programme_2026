# The Library

This is the librarian's reading list. For each subject it gives a **primary** source (what the course follows), **supplements** (alternative explanations), **problems** (extra practice), and **honours** (deeper or harder material, optional).

Key: 🆓 free and legal online · 📘 book you need to buy or borrow · 🎥 video lectures.

**Buying tip.** Most of these books are cheap as Indian or Asian low-price editions (Pearson, Cambridge, Wiley India) at Nilkhet in Dhaka or online, or borrowable from a university library. Buy the **Year 1 core** first. Buy the others one semester ahead.

## B. Problem books for the whole programme

These are for practice beyond the problem sets. They are also used to set exams.

- 📘 Morin, *Problems and Solutions in Introductory Mechanics* (Harvard). Year 1.
- 📘 Irodov, *Problems in General Physics*. Year 1–2, hard and classic. Pair it with a solutions volume, used only *after* you have made a real attempt.
- 📘 Lim Yung-Kuo (ed.), *Problems and Solutions on Mechanics / Electromagnetism / Optics / Thermodynamics & Statistical Mechanics / Quantum Mechanics / Atomic, Nuclear & Particle Physics*. These are US PhD qualifying-exam problems from "Major American Universities". Years 2–4 and the annual exams.
- 📘 Newbury et al., *Princeton Problems in Physics with Solutions*. Years 2–4.
- 📘 Cahn & Nadgorny, *A Guide to Physics Problems* (Parts 1 & 2). Years 3–4.
- 🆓 The MIT OCW past exams for each course.

**Home-lab kit** (buy during the Orientation Term, roughly BDT 6,000–9,000 total):

- Arduino Uno (or clone) starter kit with breadboard, jumpers, resistors, capacitors, LEDs, a photodiode or LDR, and a potentiometer.
- Sensors: an ultrasonic distance sensor (HC-SR04), a temperature sensor (DS18B20), an MPU-6050 accelerometer/gyroscope, and a Hall sensor.
- A digital multimeter.
- Optics: a laser pointer (red, Class 2 only), a 500–1000 lines/mm diffraction grating, polarising film sheets, and a magnifier lens.
- Mechanics: springs, a set of slotted masses or coins, a ruler, string, and a metal "slinky".
