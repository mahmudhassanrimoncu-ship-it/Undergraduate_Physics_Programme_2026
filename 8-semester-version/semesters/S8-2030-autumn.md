> **About the week numbers.** The course syllabi below are the same as in the 12-term version, but each course now runs over a 22-week semester at about two-thirds of the pace. The week numbers in the course tables have been converted: syllabus weeks 1–6 now spread over weeks 1–10, the midterm is in week 11, weeks 8–13 spread over weeks 12–19, revision is week 20 and finals are week 21. Problem sets come every two weeks per course instead of every week, so the work per course stays about the same.

## THS 402: Senior Thesis II and Defence

| Week | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 1–12 | Complete the research |
| 13–14 | Full thesis draft (10,000–15,000 words, LaTeX) |
| 15–16 | Editor's feedback; revisions |
| 19 | **Thesis submitted** |
| 20 | **Poster / public talk** (you may post the thesis publicly or on arXiv if it is good enough) |
| 22 | **Oral defence** (45 min with examiner questions), combined with the Part C viva |

**Assessment:** thesis document 60% · oral defence 25% · poster / public talk 15%. The thesis must earn **C or better** for the degree to be awarded, and **A− or better** for First Class Honours (see [assessment-and-grading.md §10](../../docs/assessment-and-grading.md)).

**Thesis marking rubric:** originality and significance 20% · correctness and rigour 30% · command of the literature 15% · quality of analysis and uncertainty 15% · clarity of writing and figures 20%.

## EXM 400: Finals (Part C, Annual Examination IV)

**Week 22 (16–22 Dec 2030).** In this version atomic, condensed-matter and nuclear and particle physics are taught in Year 4, so the Finals add one paper on them (C4).

| Paper | Content | Time |
|---|---|---|
| C1: General Physics | Synoptic questions across all four years, in the style of a PhD qualifying exam (Lim Yung-Kuo / Princeton Problems level) | 3 h |
| C2: Major Option I | OPT I | 2 h |
| C3: Major Option II | OPT II | 2 h |
| C4: Atomic, Condensed Matter, Nuclear & Particle Physics | PHY 303, PHY 304, PHY 305 | 3 h |
| Viva voce | Combined with the thesis defence | 45 min |

Once EXM 400 and THS 402 are graded, the degree is classified and the final transcript is issued.
