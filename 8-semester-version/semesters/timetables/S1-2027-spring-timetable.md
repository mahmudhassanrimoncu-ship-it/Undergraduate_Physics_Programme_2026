# Semester 1 Timetable (8-semester version): Spring 2027

**Teaching starts Monday 1 February 2027.** All times are **Bangladesh time (UTC+6)**. Course content is in [S1-2027-spring.md](../S1-2027-spring.md). The 12-term version of this timetable is [here](../../../semesters/timetables/S01-2027-spring-timetable.md), for comparison.

This semester has **4 courses over 22 weeks** (the 12-term S1 has 3 courses over 16 weeks). Each theory course has **1 lecture and 1 recitation a week** instead of 2 lectures and 1 recitation, and **problem sets are due every two weeks** per course, staggered so that two are due each Sunday.

## Ramadan and Eid

Ramadan 2027 is expected to run from about **8 February to 9 March**, with **Eid al-Fitr around 10 March**, subject to the moon sighting. That is weeks 2–6. Eid falls in week 6, five weeks before the midterms (in the 12-term version it falls the week before them). If you are fasting:

1. **Ramadan timetable.** Evening slots move to **21:30–23:30**, after tarawih, and the weekend blocks move to late morning.
2. **Eid break.** A one-week pause in week 6 (8–14 Mar). The rest of the semester moves one week later and the July break shrinks from two weeks to one, so S2 still starts on Mon 19 Jul.

## How a 5-course semester differs

S2, S3, S6 and S7 have five courses. There each theory course keeps its weekly lecture but has a recitation **every other week**, alternating with another course, so the class load stays at about 10 h a week. Problem sets are still fortnightly, so two or three are due each Sunday.
