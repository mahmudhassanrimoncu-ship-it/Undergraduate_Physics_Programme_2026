# Year 4 Major Options and Electives

All options and electives are **theory courses** with the standard weights (problem sets 25%, quizzes 5%, tutorials 10%, midterm 20%, final 40%) unless stated otherwise. Each option's final exam also forms one Part C paper. A full week-by-week syllabus is issued in the term before the course runs.

## Major options (choose two by week 12 of S9)

### OPT 4A: Astrophysics (Oxford C1)
**Prerequisites:** PHY 302, PHY 305; PHY 401 recommended. **Text:** Carroll & Ostlie.
Topics: radiative transfer · stellar structure and the equations of stellar evolution · nuclear burning · white dwarfs, neutron stars and black holes · the interstellar medium · galaxies and galactic dynamics · active galactic nuclei · exoplanet detection (TESS/Kepler data project) · large-scale structure.

### OPT 4Q: Laser Science, Quantum Optics & Quantum Information Processing (Oxford C2)
**Prerequisites:** PHY 303, PHY 402. **Texts:** Fox, *Quantum Optics*; Foot.
Topics: quantisation of the EM field · Fock, coherent and squeezed states · the Jaynes–Cummings model, cavity QED · Bloch equations · photon statistics (Hanbury Brown–Twiss) · trapped ions and neutral atoms as qubits · photonic quantum computing · quantum metrology.

### OPT 4C: Advanced Condensed Matter (Oxford C3)
**Prerequisites:** PHY 304. **Text:** Girvin & Yang; Ashcroft & Mermin.
Topics: second quantisation · Fermi liquids · BCS superconductivity · magnetism and spin waves · the quantum Hall effects · Berry phase and topological insulators · graphene and 2D materials · a computational project (DFT or tight-binding).

### OPT 4P: Particle Physics (Oxford C4)
**Prerequisites:** PHY 305. **Text:** Thomson, *Modern Particle Physics*.
Topics: the Dirac equation · QED calculations (e⁺e⁻ → μ⁺μ⁻) · deep inelastic scattering and partons · QCD · the weak interaction and the V−A structure · electroweak unification and the Higgs · neutrino physics · a CMS open-data analysis project.

### OPT 4E: Physics of Atmospheres, Oceans & Climate (Oxford C5)
**Prerequisites:** PHY 306, PHY 302. **Text:** Vallis; Pierrehumbert.
Topics: atmospheric thermodynamics · radiative balance and the greenhouse effect · rotating fluids, geostrophy, Rossby waves · ocean circulation · climate feedbacks and sensitivity · a reanalysis-data project (ERA5).

### OPT 4T: Theoretical Physics (Oxford C6)
**Prerequisites:** PHY 301, PHY 204, PHY 205; ELE 410 recommended. **Text:** Lancaster & Blundell; Tong's QFT notes.
Topics: classical field theory and Noether's theorem · canonical quantisation of the scalar field · path integrals in QM and field theory · Feynman rules and perturbation theory · the Dirac field · symmetry breaking and the Goldstone theorem · an introduction to renormalisation · the link to statistical field theory.

### OPT 4B: Biological Physics (Oxford C7)
**Prerequisites:** PHY 302, PHY 306. **Text:** Nelson; Phillips et al.
Topics: low-Reynolds-number life · diffusion and random walks in cells · entropic forces and polymer elasticity · molecular motors · membranes and electrostatics in solution · nerve impulses · gene regulation as dynamics · single-molecule data analysis.
