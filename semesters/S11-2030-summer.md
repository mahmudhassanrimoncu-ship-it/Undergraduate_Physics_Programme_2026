## THS 401: Senior Thesis I

**Modelled on:** MIT 8.THU, Princeton's senior thesis, and the Oxford MPhys project (about one term of full-time-equivalent work).
**Prerequisites:** IND 302, plus a thesis proposal approved at the end of S10.

A thesis must contain **original work**. Realistic forms for an independent researcher are:

- **Computational or theoretical:** a numerical study of a model system, or an analytic extension of a published calculation.
- **Open-data analysis:** a new analysis of LIGO, CERN, SDSS, TESS/Kepler, Gaia or Planck data.
- **Experimental:** an extension of the LAB 401 project to research quality.

| Week | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 1 | Kick-off: refine the plan and set up the repository (`work/thesis/`) |
| 2–5 | Literature review (written chapter, about 3,000 words) |
| 6 | **Proposal and literature review** submitted (graded) |
| 7–12 | Research; weekly progress meetings (supervisor role) |
| 12 | **Progress report** (graded) |
| 15 | **Interim results presentation** (25 min plus questions) |

**Assessment:** proposal and literature review 25% · progress report 25% · interim presentation 25% · supervisor assessment of progress (effort, independence, rigour) 25%.
