**Teaching starts Monday 1 February 2027.** All times are **Bangladesh time (UTC+6)**. Course content is in [S01-2027-spring.md](../S01-2027-spring.md).

This is your **class schedule**, the same as a university timetable. A slot is when a class is *scheduled*. You can attend it live with me at that time, or later the same day or week if your CSE work clashes. Either way, the slot tells you what is happening that day and keeps the week in rhythm. See [How lectures work](../../lectures/README.md).

**Weekly totals:** lectures, recitations and labs 10 h · tutorials and office hours 2.5 h · scheduled problem-set blocks 7.5 h · **about 20 h**.

**What each slot is:**
- **Lecture (80 min).** I teach the topic as a structured lecture with derivations, worked examples and concept checks. The matching MIT OCW video and textbook pages are given as your second source. Each lecture's notes are saved to the repo afterwards.
- **Recitation (60 min).** This is MIT's name for a problem-solving class. It opens with the 10-minute weekly **quiz**, then we work problems like the ones on the problem set, with you doing most of the work.
- **CMP Lecture / Lab.** On Tuesday I introduce the numerical method. On Thursday you code it on a physics problem, with me as lab TA.
- **Tutorial (Sat).** Oxford-style. You explain your solutions to last week's graded problem set and I ask follow-up questions. These count for 10% of the grade.
- **Problem-set blocks.** Protected time for homework. Hints are allowed (see the honour code), but full solutions are not.
- **Office hours.** Bring anything you're stuck on.

## Ramadan and Eid (please confirm)

Ramadan 2027 is expected to run from about **8 February to 9 March**, with **Eid al-Fitr around 10 March**. Exact dates depend on the moon sighting. With the new start date (Mon 1 Feb) that falls in **weeks 2–6**, and Eid lands in week 6, the week before the midterms. If you are fasting, two adjustments are available:

1. **Ramadan timetable.** Evening slots move to **21:30–23:30**, after tarawih, and the weekend blocks move to late morning.
2. **Eid break.** A one-week pause in Eid week (week 6, 8–14 Mar). The rest of the semester shifts by one week, so the midterms move to 22–28 Mar, S1 ends on 30 May and S2 starts on 7 Jun.

Nothing changes until you say which option, if either, you want. Any change is logged in the [Handbook changelog](../../docs/handbook.md).
