## PHY 103: Vibrations, Waves & Optics

**Modelled on:** MIT 8.03, Harvard Physics 15c, Oxford CP4 (waves) and A2 (optics), Dhaka PH 201 and PH 205.
**Prerequisites:** PHY 102, MTH 102. MTH 103 and MTH 104 concurrently.
**Description:** Oscillators, coupled oscillators and normal modes; continuous systems and the wave equation; Fourier analysis; dispersion, wave packets and group velocity; reflection and transmission; electromagnetic waves and polarisation; geometric optics; interference and diffraction. This course is the bridge to quantum mechanics.

**Primary:** Georgi, *The Physics of Waves* (free, Harvard 15c) and Hecht, *Optics* (chapters 4–10).
**Lectures:** MIT OCW 8.03SC Physics III: Vibrations and Waves.
**Supplements:** French, *Vibrations and Waves*; Morin's waves notes; Crawford, *Waves*.

| Week | Topic | Reading | Assessed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Simple harmonic motion, complex exponentials, damping | Georgi 1 | PS1 |
| 2 | Forced oscillations, resonance, the transient and the steady state | Georgi 2 | PS2 |
| 3 | Coupled oscillators, normal modes, the eigenvalue problem | Georgi 3–4 | PS3 |
| 4 | Translation invariance, beaded strings, the continuum limit | Georgi 5–6 | PS4 |
| 5 | The wave equation, standing waves, Fourier series | Georgi 7, 8 | PS5 |
| 6 | Boundaries, reflection and transmission, impedance matching | Georgi 9 | PS6 |
| 7 | **Midterm** | – | Midterm |
| 8 | Fourier transforms, wave packets, dispersion, phase and group velocity | Georgi 10–11 | PS7 |
| 9 | 2D and 3D waves; EM waves from Maxwell; energy and momentum of waves | Georgi 12–13 | PS8 |
| 10 | Polarisation, Fresnel equations, Brewster's angle | Hecht 4, 8 | PS9 + **home experiment H1** (Malus's law with phone light sensor and polarising film) |
| 11 | Geometric optics, lenses, matrix (ray-transfer) methods | Hecht 5–6 | PS10 |
| 12 | Interference: two-slit, thin films, Michelson, coherence | Hecht 9 | PS11 |
| 13 | Diffraction: Fraunhofer single and multiple slit, gratings, resolution | Hecht 10 | **Home experiment H2** (laser wavelength from a diffraction grating) |
| 14–15 | Revision and **final exam** | – | Final |

**Assessment:** problem sets 25% (H1/H2 write-ups count as problem sets) · quizzes 5% · tutorials 10% · midterm 20% · final 40%.

## MTH 103: Linear Algebra

**Modelled on:** MIT 18.06, Harvard Math 21b, Caltech Ma 1b (part), Dhaka Math-206.
**Prerequisites:** MTH 101. **ASE available.**
**Description:** Linear algebra for quantum mechanics: vector spaces, linear maps, bases, inner products, orthogonality, eigenvalues, diagonalisation, the spectral theorem for symmetric and Hermitian matrices, and SVD. It ends with complex vector spaces and function spaces as a preview of Hilbert space.

**Primary:** Strang, *Introduction to Linear Algebra* (6th ed.).
**Lectures:** MIT OCW 18.06SC (Strang).
**Supplements:** 3Blue1Brown, *Essence of Linear Algebra*; Axler, *Linear Algebra Done Right* (honours, free).

| Week | Topic | Assessed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Vectors, linear combinations, the geometry of linear equations | PS1 |
| 2 | Elimination, LU, inverses | PS2 |
| 3 | Vector spaces, subspaces, the four fundamental subspaces | PS3 |
| 4 | Independence, basis, dimension, rank | PS4 |
| 5 | Orthogonality, projections, least squares (links to LAB 101) | PS5 |
| 6 | Gram–Schmidt, QR, orthogonal matrices | PS6 |
| 7 | **Midterm** | Midterm |
| 8 | Determinants | PS7 |
| 9 | Eigenvalues and eigenvectors, diagonalisation | PS8 |
| 10 | Symmetric matrices, the spectral theorem, positive definiteness | PS9 |
| 11 | Complex vectors, Hermitian and unitary matrices, Dirac notation preview | PS10 |
| 12 | SVD, principal components | PS11 |
| 13 | Linear transformations, change of basis; function spaces and Fourier as an orthogonal basis | Quiz |
| 14–15 | Revision and **final exam** | Final |

**Assessment:** problem sets 25% · quizzes 5% · tutorials 10% · midterm 20% · final 40%.

## MTH 104: Differential Equations

**Modelled on:** MIT 18.03, Oxford CP3/CP4 (ODEs), Caltech Ma 2 (part), Dhaka Math-203.
**Prerequisites:** MTH 102. MTH 103 concurrently. **ASE available.**
**Description:** First- and second-order linear ODEs, resonance, Fourier series, Laplace transforms, the delta function and Green's function, linear systems and the matrix exponential, nonlinear systems and the phase plane, and a first look at the heat and wave PDEs solved by separation of variables.

**Primary:** MIT 18.03 notes (Miller, Orloff) with Boas ch. 7–8 and 13.
**Lectures:** MIT OCW 18.03SC Differential Equations.
**Supplements:** Tenenbaum & Pollard; Strogatz, *Nonlinear Dynamics and Chaos* ch. 5–6.

| Week | Topic | Assessed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | First-order ODEs: direction fields, separable, linear, integrating factors | PS1 |
| 2 | Numerical methods and existence/uniqueness (links to CMP 101) | PS2 |
| 3 | Complex numbers and exponentials; second-order linear ODEs with constant coefficients | PS3 |
| 4 | Inhomogeneous equations: undetermined coefficients, resonance, frequency response | PS4 |
| 5 | Fourier series; solving ODEs with periodic forcing | PS5 |
| 6 | The delta function, impulse response, convolution, Green's functions | PS6 |
| 7 | **Midterm** | Midterm |
| 8 | Laplace transforms | PS7 |
| 9 | Linear systems: eigenvalue methods, the matrix exponential | PS8 |
| 10 | Phase portraits, stability, linearisation | PS9 |
| 11 | Nonlinear systems: pendulum, Lotka–Volterra, limit cycles | PS10 |
| 12 | PDEs: the heat equation by separation of variables | PS11 |
| 13 | The wave equation; d'Alembert's solution; links to PHY 103 | Quiz |
| 14–15 | Revision and **final exam** | Final |

**Assessment:** problem sets 25% · quizzes 5% · tutorials 10% · midterm 20% · final 40%.

## EXM 100: Prelims (Annual Examination I)

**Week 16 (13–19 Dec 2027).** The format follows the Oxford Prelims (CP1–CP4) and the Dhaka viva PV 102.

| Paper | Content | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Paper 1: Physics | PHY 101, PHY 102, PHY 103. Section A has 10 short questions, all compulsory. Section B has 3 long questions chosen from 6 | 3 h |
| Paper 2: Mathematical Methods | MTH 101–104. Same format | 3 h |
| Viva voce | 30 min oral covering the year's physics and your own lab and computing work | 30 min |

Weighting: papers 80%, viva 20%. You need ≥ 50% to progress to Year 2. See [assessment-and-grading.md §7](../docs/assessment-and-grading.md).
